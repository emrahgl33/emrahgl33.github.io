Data Deletion & Privacy Policy

At Kombin Moda, we respect your privacy. Our application operates without formal account registration (no email or password required) to ensure an anonymous experience.

How to Delete Your Data

Users can permanently delete their shared content and app data directly from within the application:

Main Screen → Tap Your Profile Circle → Delete Post

To delete saved outfits from your local library:

My Outfits (Kombinlerim) → Delete Icon (Trash Can)

Account & Registration Policy

Our app does not require or store personal identifiers such as your real name, phone number, or official email. Shared photos are hosted on secure Firebase servers with a unique anonymous ID.

When you use the "Delete" features within the app, all associated media files and database records are immediately and permanently purged from our servers.

What Data is Removed?

The deletion process ensures the following data is permanently erased:

  • Uploaded outfit photos (Firebase Storage)
  • Voting interactions and scores (Firestore)
  • Device-specific identifiers used for basic analytics

Notice: This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your content cannot be recovered.
