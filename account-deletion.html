Account Deletion & Data Retention

We value your privacy and provide a simple way to manage your account and data.

How to Delete Your Account

Users can permanently delete their accounts directly from within the app. To do this, please navigate to:

Settings → Account → Delete Account

What Happens to Your Data?

Once the deletion process is completed, all personal data associated with your account will be permanently removed from our servers, including:

  • Profile information
  • Messages and communications
  • Any associated content or media

Notice: This process is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
