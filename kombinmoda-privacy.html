Privacy Policy

📋 Overview

Last updated: June 2025

KombinModa is a personal fashion assistant app offering wardrobe management, body analysis, and outfit suggestions. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Short version: All personal data (photos, analyses, wardrobe information) is stored only on your device. No personal data is sent to any server. No user account is required.

📱 Data Stored On-Device Only On-Device

The following information is kept solely on your phone and is never transmitted to external servers:

  • Wardrobe photos (added from camera or gallery)
  • Body analysis results (shoulder type, waist, hip information)
  • Style and color preferences
  • Gender and age range selections
  • Outfit history and clothing categories

To delete all this data, simply uninstall the app from your device.

🌐 Third-Party Services

The app connects to external services solely to display content. No personal information is shared in these connections:

Service Purpose Data Sent
GNews API Fashion news feed Search query only (e.g. "women fashion news")
Unsplash API Trend images Search query only (e.g. "men fashion")
Firebase Analytics Anonymous usage statistics Anonymous app activity — not linked to your identity

📢 Advertising & Advertising ID Note

KombinModa uses advertising revenue to remain free of charge. In this context:

  • The Unity Ads platform is used to display ads.
  • Your device's Advertising ID (Android: GAID / iOS: IDFA) may be used for ad personalisation.
  • On iOS, you will be asked for permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt when the app is first opened.
  • If you decline, general (non-personalised) ads will be shown instead.

Unity Ads Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

👶 Children's Privacy

KombinModa is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13.
